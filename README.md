# IIM Research

Research into autonomous, self-developing artificial intelligence within the framework of **[IIM - Independent Intelligence Manifesto](https://github.com/mrmerovingian/iim)**.

Research notes, hypotheses, mathematical models, experiment code, and test results will be published here.

**Status:** preparing the first publications.

**License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Full text](./LICENSE). Applies to the materials in this repository.

**Original language:** Russian. Translations should match the Russian original. Differences in meaning can be reported through [Issues](https://github.com/mrmerovingian/iim-research/issues).

## Research directions

- **Methods of thinking and learning.** Investigating how an agent can choose, revise, and improve its own problem-solving methods.
- **Transfer of experience.** Testing which acquired skills and methods are retained when the environment changes and accessible records of events are removed.
- **Time and decision-making.** Studying how a representation of limited operating time affects task selection, resource allocation, and the transfer of results.

## Approach

Hypotheses will be distinguished from confirmed results. Experiments will be accompanied by descriptions of their conditions, control comparisons, reproduction procedures, and limitations. Errors and negative results will also be documented.

A change to a system will not, on its own, be considered evidence of improvement: any advantage must be tested.

## Manifesto

The goals and principles of the research program are set out in [IIM - Independent Intelligence Manifesto](https://github.com/mrmerovingian/iim).

Materials will be added as they become ready.
